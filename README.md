# Portfolio-Projection
The project focuses on equity research, valuation, portfolio construction, and portfolio risk analysis in the Vietnamese equity market.

## Project Overview

The project consists of two main components:

* An equity research report and valuation model for Techcombank (TCB).
* A portfolio strategy report applying portfolio management and quantitative finance methodologies.

The analysis was developed where teams acted as equity investment funds constructing a Vietnam-focused listed equity portfolio.

## Repository Structure

### 1. Techcombank Research Report

**File:** `Equity Research.pdf`

A full equity research report on Techcombank (TCB), covering:

* Business model analysis.
* Industry overview and competitive positioning.
* Corporate governance.
* Financial statement analysis.
* Profitability and asset quality assessment.
* Valuation methodology.
* Technical analysis.
* Risk assessment.

The report concludes with a BUY recommendation based on intrinsic valuation and growth outlook.

---

### 2. Techcombank Valuation Model

**File:** `Equity Research.xlsx`

Comprehensive financial model for Techcombank (TCB), including:

* Revenue and earnings forecasting.
* FCFE Discounted Cash Flow valuation.
* Comparable valuation (P/E and P/B).
* Scenario analysis.
* Sensitivity analysis.
* Financial ratio analysis.
* Target price estimation.

The model was built to support the equity research thesis and investment recommendation.

---

### 3. Portfolio Strategy Report

**File:** `Portfolio Projection.pdf`

Portfolio allocation report developed under the assumption of managing a Vietnam equity investment fund.

Main contents include:

* Investment Policy Statement (IPS).
* Macroeconomic analysis.
* Sector allocation strategy.
* Stock selection rationale.
* Black-Litterman optimized portfolio weights.
* Scenario-based portfolio projections.
* Risk management framework.
* Performance measurement and attribution.

The overall investment strategy focused on identifying high-quality Vietnamese growth equities positioned to benefit from:

* Vietnam’s macroeconomic expansion.
* Public investment acceleration.
* Emerging market reclassification potential.
* Digital transformation.
* Consumption recovery.
* FDI-driven industrial growth.

The portfolio targeted medium-to-high risk investors seeking long-term capital appreciation with disciplined risk management.

Selected sectors & stocks:

* Financials (TCB, VCB).
* Materials (HPG).
* Consumer Discretionary (MWG).
* Real Estate (SIP, KBC).

---

### 4. Portfolio Projection & Simulation

**File:** `Portfolio Projection.ipynb`

Python notebook used for portfolio analytics and quantitative modeling.

Key methodologies include:

* Black-Litterman portfolio optimization.
* Backtesting analysis using Fama-French Framework.
* Scenario analysis.
* Monte Carlo simulation.
* Portfolio return projection.
* Risk-adjusted performance measurement.
* Performance attribution.
